Billing FAQ

Questions about pricing.

Can I pay monthly?

Plans are billed every 6, 12 or 24 months — that is how we keep prices this low. The monthly figure shown is just for comparison.

How much do I save?

20% on 6 months, 30% on 12 months and 35% on 24 months, compared with the ₹ per-month list price.

Is GST included?

Prices are shown without GST; 18% GST is added on your invoice.

Can I upgrade later?

Yes. Move from Small Business to Enterprise any time — we adjust the remaining amount.

How do I pay?

Online payment is coming soon. Today, after your trial you choose a plan and pay by UPI or bank transfer; we activate it the same day.

What if I need more than 1,000 customers or 3 staff?

Choose Enterprise — no limits on customers or staff.
