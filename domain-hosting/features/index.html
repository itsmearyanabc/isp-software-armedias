01 · Customers

Customer 360 — everyone in one place

Add customers with just a name and phone. Everything else — address, plan, opening balance, ID, notes — is optional.

  1. Open Customers → Add, enter name and phone, save.
  2. Click the eye icon to see their full profile: bills, payments, complaints, documents and a timeline.
  3. Use the coloured status bar to filter active / inactive customers, and the WhatsApp or call buttons to reach them.
Old dues? Enter them as Opening balance when adding the customer — they flow into every report.

02 · Billing

GST invoices with a live calculator

Create a bill and watch the maths happen: amount, discount (₹ or %), net amount, GST (₹ or %) and total.

  1. Open Invoices → Add. Today's date and the current month fill in automatically.
  2. Pick the customer — their product and price fill in for you.
  3. Type the discount and GST as an amount or a percentage, e.g. 10% and 18%.
  4. Save. You're asked whether to send the bill on WhatsApp or email.
Bill everyone at once: Invoices → Run Billing creates this month's bill for every active customer.

03 · Counter sales

Live billing while taking payment

At the counter? Make the bill and record the payment in one go.

  1. Open Payments → Add and click Create bill (top right).
  2. Pick products (or type a new one), set quantity — the price fills in.
  3. Add discount / GST. Amount received follows the bill total; change it for part-payments.
  4. Click Save bill & payment, then send it on WhatsApp.
New items you type are saved to your product list automatically, ready for next time.

04 · Sharing

Send bills & receipts on WhatsApp in one tap

Every bill and receipt gets a permanent private link. Customers open it to view, print or save the PDF.

  1. After saving a bill, choose WhatsApp in the popup — or press the WhatsApp button on any payment.
  2. WhatsApp opens with the message and link already written. Just press send.
  3. Prefer email? Choose Email to send the PDF bill to the customer's inbox.
Enterprise can connect the official WhatsApp Business API to send PDF bills automatically from your business number.

05 · Recovery

Collect dues faster

The Recovery board lists everyone who owes you, worst first — with total due, previous dues and days overdue.

  1. Open Recovery Board. Filter by area/branch or search a name.
  2. Tap WhatsApp to send a polite reminder, or call directly.
  3. Record the payment right there. Print an A4 collection sheet for field staff.
The dashboard shows overdue bills and promised payments due today, so nothing slips.

06 · Sales

Leads, follow-ups & conversion

Track every enquiry from first contact to a paying customer.

  1. Add a lead with name, phone and source (walk-in, call, website, referral…).
  2. Move it through New → Contacted → Quoted → Negotiating → Won.
  3. Set the next follow-up date — due follow-ups appear on your dashboard.
  4. Click Convert to customer when they buy.
Website forms and ad leads can arrive automatically through the API.

07 · Team

Staff logins, roles & permissions

Give every staff member their own login and decide exactly what they can see and do.

  1. Open Staff / Users → Add and choose a role: Admin, Accountant or Collector.
  2. Owners can fine-tune any role in Roles & Permissions — View, Add, Edit, Delete per page.
  3. Every action is recorded in the Activity log.
Passwords are encrypted, and staff can reset their own with a one-time code by email.

09 · Integrations

API keys & automation

Connect your website, Facebook/Google lead ads or any app.

  1. Open API & Integrations → New API key and copy the key (shown once).
  2. In Zapier, Make or n8n add an HTTP request step and paste the endpoint and key.
  3. New leads, customers or tickets appear in your CRM instantly.
Each key works only for your business and can be revoked any time.
