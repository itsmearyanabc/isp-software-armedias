FAQ

Questions we hear often.

Is there a free trial?

Yes — every new business gets a 3-day free trial with all features, starting the moment you sign up. No card and no approval needed. You can add sample data to explore straight away.

What happens when the trial ends?

Your account locks and you choose a plan — Small Business or Enterprise. Your data stays safe; everything is exactly as you left it once you subscribe.

How does billing work?

Plans are billed every 6, 12 or 24 months. The longer you choose, the more you save: 20%, 30% or 35% off the monthly price. GST is extra.

Can my staff use it on their phones?

Yes — it works in any browser on mobile, tablet or computer. Each staff member gets their own login with the permissions you choose.

Can I send bills on WhatsApp?

Yes. Every bill and receipt has a private link that opens WhatsApp with the message ready. Enterprise can also connect the official WhatsApp Business API to send PDF bills automatically.

Is my data safe?

Each business has its own separate database, staff passwords are encrypted, every action is checked against your permissions and recorded in the activity log. You can even keep your data in your own Google Drive.

Do you build custom features?

Yes — that is what our Enterprise plan is for. AR Medias builds custom CRM, ERP, apps and automation. See Enterprise or contact us.
