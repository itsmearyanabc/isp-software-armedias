FAQ

Questions we hear often.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Sign up and we activate a free trial so you can try everything with your own data before you pay.

How does billing work?

Plans are billed every 6 months. We remind you before your plan ends, and you can renew or upgrade any time.

Can my staff use it on their phones?

Yes — it works in any browser on mobile, tablet or computer. Each staff member gets their own login with the permissions you choose.

Can I send bills on WhatsApp?

Yes. Connect your WhatsApp Business number through the official Meta WhatsApp API to send PDF bills, or use the free click-to-chat option. Email bills are included too.

Is my data safe?

Each business has its own separate database. Logins are checked on every action, staff only see what you allow, and every change is recorded in the activity log.

Can I bring my existing customers?

Yes — add them with their old balances, and our team can help you import them when you start.
